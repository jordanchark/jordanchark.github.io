In some cases, a logical expression can be signaled by one or more overt morphemes, apparently without a change in meaning.  This project investigates the hypothesis that optional morphological doubling is cross-linguistically correlated with a particular situational-functional property of discourse – namely, to what extent a unique Question Under Discussion is retrievable – and thus to register. 

Current phenomena of interest include doubled comparatives (*more taller*) and doubled universal quantifiers (*everyone-everyone*).
